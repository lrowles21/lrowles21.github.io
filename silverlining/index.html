About Us

A small forge with a big fire

MetaForge is an independent studio founded by Landon Rowles and Collin Bria — builders first, with a simple conviction: VR's best moments are social, and the next great VR game will be a place people want to be, not just a thing they play.

We work small and ship real: hand-painted concept art, Unreal Engine 5, and a production pipeline that goes from sketch to playable space fast. Every piece of the world — from the bazaar shops to the Great Tree — is designed by hand, built to be readable in VR, and warm enough to come home to.

The Game

Laser tag, reborn as fantasy — in zero G

Aboard the Silver Lining — a magical vessel sailing the stars — adventurers gather in a warm tavern hub, swear themselves to a class, and step through portals into zero-gravity arenas where teams of five battle for glory. Push off walls. Ride your momentum. Win with your friends.

Choose Your Class

Five classes. One ritual.

No menus — you choose who you are at the Statue Hall, by hand. Warrior, Wizard, Cleric, Ranger, Assassin: each with a light move, a heavy move, and an ultimate. Easy to learn. A lifetime to master.

The Signature Mechanic

Frozen, never fallen

Nobody dies in the Silver Lining's arenas — the defeated freeze. And frozen teammates are part of the battlefield: carry them as shields, hurl them across the arena, or throw them behind enemy lines so they thaw exactly where the fight needs them. Defeat isn't a timeout. It's a tactic.

I

Social First

The hub is the heart. Matches are what friends do together between conversations.

II

No Menus

Everything is physical — oracle stones, statues, rituals. The world is the interface.

III

Readable Combat

Stylized and clear by design. If it hurts clarity, it doesn't ship.

IV

No Pay-to-Win

Cosmetics are identity, never advantage. Skill is the only currency that wins.
